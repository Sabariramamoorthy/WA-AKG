Invoice Template Master

Stock Invoicerr has no template catalog. This page is the Sindhu B2B / B2C master for the live PDF overlay.

How templates are chosen

On Invoices, use the green floating panel: B2B Tax or B2C Retail. That writes [[TEMPLATE:B2B]] or [[TEMPLATE:B2C]] into invoice Notes. The PDF renderer picks the layout from that tag (or buyer GSTIN for B2B).

PDF layouts

B2B Tax Invoice GST tax invoice layout for registered buyers (GSTIN on client). B2C Retail Invoice Retail / consumer invoice when buyer has no GSTIN.

Change invoice status

  1. Draft → Unpaid (issue): open the invoice PDF/detail URL, then floating panel → Issue (Draft → Unpaid). Removes the Draft watermark.
  2. Unpaid → Paid: floating panel → Mark as Paid, or Invoices list → payment / Mark as paid (creates a Payment).
  3. Send: floating panel → Send by email (needs SMTP), or list → Send by email (sets Sent).
  4. Archive: only after Paid — use Invoices list archive action.

Create form tip

The stock create-invoice dialog has no “template type” dropdown. Before saving a new invoice, click B2B Tax or B2C Retail on the floating panel so Notes gets the tag. Empty Notes auto-prefills your last choice.

Also in Settings

Company PDF colors / email bodies live under Settings (PDF template + email templates). Those are not a multi-layout invoice master — B2B/B2C lives here and in the PDF overlay.
